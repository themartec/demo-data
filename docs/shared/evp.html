Cascadia Cloud Systems — Employee Value Proposition

Meaningful technical challenges

Engineers work on distributed systems handling real production scale — order-fulfilment, ranking, and platform infrastructure used by paying customers, not internal toy projects.

Remote-first flexibility

Most engineering roles are remote-friendly with a small overlap window for synchronous collaboration. Work from wherever you do your best work.

Growth and mentorship

Every engineer is paired with a mentor in their first month, and there is a defined technical ladder through to Staff and Principal, not just a management track.

Competitive total rewards

Above-market base pay, meaningful equity, and an annual learning budget engineers can spend on courses, conferences or books with no approval friction.

Low-ego, high-trust culture

Blameless postmortems, decisions made in the open, and a norm of engineers pushing back on their own leads in design review without it being a career risk.
